# Chapter 5 flags for blind players

## 2026-11-12 (drafted by hand by Claude Code; from the 10 Nov survey plus screenshots)

### Room 45 (castle towers)
- Every tower is an exit but the engine gives only stock filler on look, so a player hears four identical "towers". The draft labels them by position.
- Tower right of centre (103) is hidden at the start and appears later, so the list grows mid-chapter.

### Room 49 (werewolf's study)
- Two exits (109, 110) both lead to room 45 and are both silent. Labelled left and right from the picture only.
- Walking in the first time starts a long talk; a player pressing keys during it may think the game is stuck.
- Diploma (202) gives stock filler only.

### Rooms 50, 51, 52 (inside the towers)
- Every door is silent. Room 50 has two exits back to 45.
- Positions for 107, 112 and 114 were not dumped; inferred from the pictures.

### Room 53 (laboratory)
- Plug (120) and switch (123) give stock filler only, and both are puzzle objects.
- The plug blocker (the a11y layer drops the plug) was fixed in 56c2080; not rechecked here.

### Room 54 (dungeon)
- Two hotspots share 118 (cell door and its window).
- Looking at the cell door opens a conversation through it that must be finished first.
- Bone (9999) is hidden at the start and gives stock filler only.

### Room 55 (corridor with the sign)
- The barred gate (206) is not an exit. Any verb on it plays an animation and walks you back, with no speech at all. A blind player hears only a sound effect.
- The sign is in Spanish (AQUI NO DUERME DRASCULA); translated in the draft.

### Room 57 (behind the gate)
- Same picture as room 55, so it may be confused with it unless the name says where you are.

### Room 56 (bedroom)
- Clock (207) gives stock filler only.
- Opening the coffin (124) ends the chapter.

### Inventory
- Items 9, 20 and 28 had no chapter 4 or 5 entry; drafted in drafts/items/items-ch4-ch5.json.
